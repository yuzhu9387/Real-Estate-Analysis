Selection summary
Build-suitability score (0–100) is MY heuristic, not ATTOM data — high = small vacant infill with residential zoning; low = huge open-space/watershed, public-works, agriculture/hills, or whole-area parcels (unsuitable for homes). General Plan land-use & live current-use/on-market are NOT in ATTOM. Public land is tax-exempt so many assessed values are 0. Atherton & Menlo Park (San Mateo Co.) are outside this dataset.
